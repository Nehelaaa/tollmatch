TollMatch

Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

The short version

TollMatch works entirely inside your own Chrome browser. Your toll history, your guests' trip details and your settings are stored on your computer only. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and nothing is sold or shared.

What TollMatch uses

  • Your E-ZPass transaction history, which you export from your E-ZPass account and add yourself. It includes toll times, locations, transponder numbers, plates and amounts.
  • Your car settings: which transponders and plates belong to your rental cars and to your own car, and the exit you use to get home.
  • Trip details from your own Turo host account: guest first names, reservation numbers, trip start and end times, handover location and toll invoices already sent. TollMatch reads these from turo.com pages while you are signed in.
  • A bookings sheet, if you upload one: guest names, contact details you include, plates, and pickup and return times.

How it is used

  • Only to work out which tolls happened while each guest had your car, and to prepare the toll statement and message for that guest.
  • When you click to send, TollMatch fills in the toll invoice on turo.com in your browser. It sends an invoice only after you approve it, and only through your own Turo account.

Where it is stored

Everything is kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer. You can delete it at any time with Remove in TollMatch, or by removing the extension.

What TollMatch does not do

  • It has no server and no account. Your data is never uploaded to the developer.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Your data is not sold, transferred to third parties, used for anything except the purpose above, or used to decide creditworthiness or for lending.
  • It runs no code downloaded from the internet. Everything it needs is included in the extension.

Guests' information

As a host you are responsible for how you use your guests' information. TollMatch only helps you bill guests for tolls from their own trips.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub, or use the contact email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
