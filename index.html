TollMatch

Bill every guest the right tolls

A Chrome extension for car-sharing hosts.

What it does

  • Matches your E-ZPass toll history to each guest's trip times. Reads exports from any E-ZPass state: CSV, Excel, PDF statements or a pasted table.
  • Leaves out your own delivery and pickup drives automatically, and shows why each toll was kept or left out.
  • Checks all your Turo trips at once and lists who still needs a toll invoice.
  • Prepares the toll statement and message, and fills in the invoice when you approve it.
  • Not on Turo? Upload a bookings spreadsheet instead.

Your data stays with you

Everything runs in your browser. See the privacy policy.
