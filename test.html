How to test TollMatch

For Chrome Web Store reviewers. All sample data is made up.

1. Download the sample files

  • sample-ezpass-history.csv (made-up E-ZPass toll history)
  • sample-bookings.xlsx (two made-up bookings)

2. Load the tolls

Click the TollMatch icon to open the side panel. Drop sample-ezpass-history.csv on "Drop your E-ZPass CSV here". It shows 13 tolls loaded.

3. Set the cars

Transponder 1234567 is pre-marked "Rental car": type the plate DEMO42. Leave 7654321 as "My own car". Click Save cars.

4. Check the bookings

In step 3 choose My own bookings, click Upload bookings and pick sample-bookings.xlsx.

Expected: Jane Doe .25 and Sam Lee .40. Click Jane Doe: her host's delivery drive and the pickup after she returned are crossed out, with the reason under each toll. "Download statement" saves the toll statement image, and "Copy message" copies the message for the guest.

About the Turo options

"All Turo trips" and "One Turo trip" only work for a signed-in Turo host on turo.com. They read that host's own trips and fill in Turo's toll invoice form only after the host approves each send. All data stays in the browser's local extension storage.
